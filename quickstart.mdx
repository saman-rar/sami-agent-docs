This page walks you through everything you need to get started with Sami: opening the app, signing in with your Vercel account, sending your first message, and trying one of Sami's built-in skills. The whole process takes less than five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sami">
    Navigate to your Sami deployment URL in any modern browser. If you are using a hosted instance, your team or administrator will have shared the URL with you. If you deployed Sami yourself, this is the production URL Vercel assigned to your project.

    You land on the sign-in page, which shows the **sami** name, a green **Ready** indicator, and a **Continue with Vercel** button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in with Vercel">
    Click **Continue with Vercel**. The button label changes to **Redirecting…** while the request is in flight, then your browser navigates to Vercel's authorization page.

    On the Vercel authorization page, review the requested permissions and click **Allow**. Vercel redirects you back to Sami and creates a new session. The entire flow typically takes a few seconds.

    Once you are signed in, your avatar appears in the top-left corner and the chat interface opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a message">
    Type any message into the prompt box at the center of the screen and press **Enter**, or click the submit button on the right side of the prompt box.

    Sami streams its reply back token by token — you will see the text appear progressively rather than all at once. While a response is streaming, a **Stop** button replaces the submit button so you can cancel at any time.

    Your conversation moves to a unique URL under `/s/<session-id>`. You can bookmark this URL and return to the conversation later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Try a skill">
    Sami automatically applies the right skill based on what you ask. To see this in action, try one of the following prompts:

    - `Help me write a GitHub PR description for my latest changes.`
    - `How do I add a tool to my eve agent?`
    - `Edit this paragraph for clarity: [paste your text]`
    - `How do I add a Dialog component using shadcn/ui?`

    Sami recognizes the task and brings in the relevant skill knowledge — PR structure rules, eve framework docs, writing style guidelines, or shadcn/ui component patterns — without any extra configuration on your part.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a new session">
    Once you are in an active conversation, a **New chat** button appears in the top-right corner of the header. Click it to open a fresh session with a new URL. Your previous sessions remain available at their original URLs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Each session gets a unique URL you can bookmark and return to at any time. Sessions persist across browser refreshes and tab closes, so you will not lose your conversation if you navigate away.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Self-hosted developers:** When you run Sami locally in development mode, the Vercel OAuth sign-in flow is bypassed so you can test without completing authorization. This only applies to local development (`NODE_ENV=development`) and does not affect any deployed instance.
</Note>
