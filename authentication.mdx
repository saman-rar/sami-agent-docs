Sami uses Vercel as its identity provider. When you open Sami for the first time — or after your session expires — you see a sign-in page instead of the chat interface. This page explains how to complete the sign-in flow, what your session represents, and how to sign out when you are done.

<Note>
  If you are not signed in, Sami shows the sign-in page and you cannot access the chat. Every route in Sami requires a valid session.
</Note>

## Signing in

The sign-in page displays three pieces of information: the **sami** agent name, a green **Ready** indicator confirming the service is available, and a **Continue with Vercel** button.

To sign in:

1. Click **Continue with Vercel**. The button label changes to **Redirecting…** while your browser navigates to Vercel's authorization page.
2. On Vercel's authorization page, review the requested permissions and click **Allow**.
3. Vercel redirects you back to Sami and your session begins automatically. The chat interface opens and your avatar appears in the top-left corner.

If sign-in fails — for example because of a network error or a declined authorization — Sami shows a **Sign-in failed. Try again.** message beneath the button. Click the button again to retry.

## Your session

After sign-in, Sami creates a session that ties your identity to the current browser. Sessions last **8 hours** from the time they are created. Session lifetime is fixed and does not reset with activity — after 8 hours, Sami prompts you to sign in again.

While your session is active, you can:

- Send messages and receive streaming responses.
- Navigate between conversations using their unique URLs.
- Close and reopen the browser tab without losing your place.

Your name and avatar are visible in the top-left corner of the chat interface for the duration of the session.

## Account menu

Your avatar in the top-left corner opens the account menu. The menu shows:

- Your **display name** in medium-weight text.
- Your **email address** in muted text beneath your name.
- A **Log out** option with a log-out icon, separated from your account details by a divider.

Click anywhere outside the menu to close it without taking any action.

## Signing out

To sign out of Sami:

1. Click your avatar in the top-left corner to open the account menu.
2. Click **Log out**.

The button label changes to **Logging out…** while the request is processed. Once complete, your browser returns to the sign-in page and your session is invalidated. Any unsaved conversation state visible in that tab is cleared, though your previous sessions remain accessible at their URLs if you sign in again.
